Privacy Policy

A Day With Jesus · Last updated November 12, 2026

In short: your notes, prayers, sermons and files belong to you. They’re stored on your device and, if you sign in, in your own Google Drive. We don’t sell, share or look at your data, and there are no ads or trackers.

Who we are

A Day With Jesus is a free devotional app run by Shilrick Monsuller. For questions or data requests, use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen, or contact us on GitHub.

What the app stores

  • On your device: your reading progress, memory verse progress, saved verses, notes, personal prayers, sermons, Bible study notes, settings and any files you attach. This stays in your browser’s storage on that device.
  • In your Google Drive (only if you sign in): a copy of the same data, kept in a hidden app folder that only this app can open. It doesn’t appear in your Drive file list, and only you can access it.
  • Shared groups (only if you join or create one): prayer meetings and group or one-on-one Bible studies are stored in a database (Google Firebase) so that members can see each other’s items. This includes the group name, members’ Gmail addresses, prayer items, session notes, shared notes, and the names and links of shared files. Only members of a group can read it.
  • Files you share in a Bible study are uploaded to your own Google Drive, in a folder named “A Day With Jesus – shared files,” and set so that anyone with the link can view them. The link is shown only to members of that study. You can delete these files at any time.

Google sign-in

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for your name, email address and profile picture, and for two limited Google Drive permissions:

  • drive.appdata: to save and read the app’s own data in a private app folder in your Drive.
  • drive.file: to upload and manage only the files you choose to share through the app. The app can’t see your other Drive files.

Your Google sign-in is used only to sync your data and to show your name in groups you belong to. The app’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your data, or share it with advertisers.
  • We don’t use analytics, ads or tracking cookies.
  • We don’t read your personal notes, prayers or files.

Other services

“NIV” and “RCPV” buttons open BibleGateway and YouVersion in your browser; those sites have their own privacy policies. Google provides sign-in, Google Drive and Firebase under Google’s Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

  • On a device: open Settings → Sign out & remove from this device, or clear the site’s data in your browser.
  • In Google Drive: go to Drive settings → Manage apps, find A Day With Jesus, and choose Delete hidden app data. You can also remove the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • In shared groups: delete your own items in the app, or ask the group’s leader. To have your email removed from all groups, contact us using the details above.

Children

The app is meant for general audiences and doesn’t knowingly collect information from children under 13 without a parent’s involvement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
